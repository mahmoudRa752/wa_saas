# Administrator Operations Guide

This guide explains how company administrators manage team members, configurations, and campaigns.

## 1. Employee Management
- Navigate to **Employees**.
- Add employees with name, email, password, and custom WhatsApp Phone Number ID.
- Link or unlink WhatsApp phone IDs in **Settings -> WhatsApp Integration**.

## 2. Broadcast Campaigns
- Navigate to **Broadcast Campaigns**.
- Enter Campaign Name and Message body.
- Upload Excel/CSV spreadsheet contacts list (with numbers in the first column) or list them manually.
- Schedule the campaign date and time.
- Cancel or track active campaign progress from the list table.

## 3. Auto-Replies & Automation
- Navigate to **Auto-Replies**.
- Add automation rules matching keyword content or setting global auto-responders.
- Toggle rule status to Muted or Active.

## 4. Performance Metrics & Audit Logs
- Navigate to **Performance** to review team leaderboards, messages volume, and average response times.
- Navigate to **Audit Logs** to review a timeline of employee additions, live chat assignment edits, status modifications, and campaign schedules.
